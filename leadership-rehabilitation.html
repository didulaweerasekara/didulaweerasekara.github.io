COUNSELLING / COMMUNITY SERVICE

Psychosocial Support & Rehabilitation

Ambepussa Rehabilitation Centre · Sri Lanka

Role: Volunteer Counsellor

I served as a volunteer counsellor in a rehabilitation programme at the Ambepussa Rehabilitation Centre, in collaboration with Mental Health University Sri Lanka and Prof. Gnanadasa Perera.

My involvement included group-based and creative activities within the rehabilitation setting. This experience reflects my commitment to psychosocial wellbeing, human dignity and community service.
