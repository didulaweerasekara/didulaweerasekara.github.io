02 / MY STORY

Education beyond the classroom.

Are we educating people merely to acquire knowledge, or are we preparing them to understand the world and contribute to it?

This is a question I often find myself asking as an educator. My journey began with English language teaching and gradually extended into educational leadership, psychology and communication. These experiences have influenced the way I understand people and society.

Through VOXDORA, I explore ideas with people whose perspectives deserve to be heard. Through COUNTERGAZE, I explore the stories and assumptions behind what we see.

I do not claim to have all the answers. What interests me is the process of finding them, questioning them and sharing what I discover along the way.

06 / LOOKING AHEAD

Knowledge. People. Transformation.

To contribute to the development of a generation of intellectually independent, ethically responsible and socially conscious individuals capable of shaping a better society.

To create opportunities for intellectual, communicative and leadership development through education, research and meaningful public discourse.
