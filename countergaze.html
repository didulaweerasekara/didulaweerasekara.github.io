VISUAL ESSAY / COLLECTION 001

Where Innocent Lives Float

Colombo, Sri Lanka · November 2026
PHOTOGRAPHER'S STATEMENT

It was an emotional morning in what we often call the commercial heart of Sri Lanka.

I had barely got off the bus when I witnessed a woman shouting, blaming those around her. What struck me was the apparent contradiction in her behaviour. She seemed to be looking down upon the homeless while appearing to experience circumstances not entirely different from theirs. Her words were filled with accusations, some directed towards the armed forces. What lay behind her distress, I could not tell.

A little further along, I noticed people sleeping on the pavement.

Then, a green garment hanging from a wooden beam caught my attention. Its design reminded me of traditional Tamil attire, although I knew nothing about its owner. Further below, a sarong appeared to serve as a makeshift wall.

These seemingly insignificant objects made me think about the coexistence we so frequently speak of. What does coexistence mean when some of us do not even have a place to call home?

Against this reality stood the symbols of a prosperous Colombo — the Lotus Tower, Shangri-La, One Galle Face and ITC Ratnadipa.

Two realities, existing within the same city, yet seemingly worlds apart.

I wondered what development meant to those lying on the pavement beneath that skyline. Do they see the same Colombo that we celebrate?

Once, this was the Floating Market.

Today, it seems that innocent lives are left to float in the very market we once celebrated.
